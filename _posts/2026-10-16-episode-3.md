Joel and Greg interview Sam Ruby, known for leading the Atom feed standard, building the Feed Validator, his leadership at the Apache Software Foundation, co-chairing the W3C HTML Working Group during HTML5, and co-authoring *Agile Web Development with Rails*.

Sam explains how a ballroom dance competition scheduling mishap led him to build an app, and eventually Roundhouse: a compiler-like system that treats Rails as a language and can transpile Rails apps into targets like Rust, Go, TypeScript, and native binaries, so they can deploy on platforms like Vercel and Cloudflare. He walks through the path from Ruby2JS to Railcar to Roundhouse, demos including Campfire compiled to a single native binary, working with Matz on Spinel through agent-driven pull requests, and growing interest that reaches all the way to Shopify Core.

The conversation also covers using LLM agents as a supervisory workflow, why rigor through references, tests, and specs matters, Rails as a 4GL, what all this means for newcomers, and how agents are speeding up open source onboarding.
