# YouTube thumbnail style

What the hosts like (and don't) in episode thumbnails. `/pingback-deploy`
reads this before making options and adds to it after each pick. Keep it to
things a host actually said or chose.

## Format
- 1280×720 JPEG, under 2 MB (YouTube's limit). Saved as
  `assets/images/episodes/episode-N.jpg`, which the site also uses for cards
  and the video facade.
- Readable at phone size: a few big words at most.

## So far
Three different directions; no house style picked yet.
- **Episode 1:** both hosts on their own backgrounds, Greg pointing at the
  middle; big white "Episode 1" with "PINGBACK: Ruby, Tech, & More" under it;
  the pink ping-wave gem in the centre.
- **Episode 2:** busy YouTube style: blue grid, "PINGBACK PODCAST" in white
  caps, yellow "EPISODE 2" banner, "RAUL ON RAILS" in white and orange, a ruby
  and a cyan arrow pointing at three faces in panels.
- **Episode 3:** dark and minimal: navy background, the guest's name huge in
  white ("Sam") and ruby pink ("Ruby"), one face cut out with a soft glow, the
  small cover art top left.
